1.10 Stakeholders

These are people who have a stake or interest in the project. Their early identification is important as you need

to set up adcquate communication channels with them. Stakeholders can be categorized as:

e Internal to the project team This means that they will be under the direct managerial control of the
project leader,

e External to the project team but within the same organization For example, the project leader might
need the assistance of the users to carry out systems testing. Here the commitment of the people
involved has to be negotiated.

e External to both the project team and the organization External stakeholders may be customers (or
users) who will benefit from the system that the project implements. They may be contractors who will
carry out work for the project. The relationship here is usually based on a contract.
